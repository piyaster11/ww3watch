        What should a normal person do?

        • Don't panic-sell or panic-buy. Markets price war risk faster than you can trade it. Stick to your plan.
        • Expect higher fuel and travel costs while Hormuz stays closed — budget for it, and check flights through the Gulf before you book.
        • Travelling? Check your government's advisory and register with your embassy (Thai MFA: mfa.go.th, US: travel.state.gov).
        • Keep a basic 72-hour kit (water, cash, medicine, power bank) — useful for floods and blackouts too.
        • Check before you share. Most "WW3 tonight" posts are recycled or fake.

        Methodology

        Nine signals are each scored 1–10 and combined by weight (shown on each row). Live signals refresh automatically every day at ~07:00 Bangkok: Prediction markets = combined Polymarket odds of five major escalation events (0% → 1, 40%+ → 10); Market fear = average 1-year percentile of VIX, gold, Brent and the ITA defense ETF (Yahoo Finance); Travel bans = count of US State Dept Level-4 countries above a chronic baseline of ~18, plus embassy departures. Curated signals are scored by an analyst from linked reporting and dated. Bands: 1–2.4 Calm · 2.5–4.4 Tense · 4.5–6.4 Elevated · 6.5–8.4 Danger · 8.5–10 Brink. If a live source fails, its last value is kept and marked stale.

        30-day chart: points from 1 Nov 2026 onward are live (recorded by the daily 07:00 refresh). Earlier points are reconstructed: market fear from real Yahoo daily closes, prediction markets from Polymarket's public daily price history, curated signals from dated reporting (ISW, JINSA, CBS, Al Jazeera) carried forward between documented change points, and travel advisories carried at today's value because the State Dept feed is not dated per change. Note: the "ceasefire breaks before midterms" market loses risk mechanically as 3 Nov approaches.
