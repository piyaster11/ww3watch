      Methodology

      Nine signals are each scored 1–10 and combined by weight (shown on each row). Live signals refresh automatically every day at ~07:00 Bangkok: Prediction markets = combined Polymarket odds of five major escalation events (0% → 1, 40%+ → 10); Market fear = average 1-year percentile of VIX, gold, Brent and the ITA defense ETF (Yahoo Finance); Travel bans = count of US State Dept Level-4 countries above a chronic baseline of ~18, plus embassy departures. Curated signals are scored by an analyst from linked reporting and dated. Bands: 1–2.4 Calm · 2.5–4.4 Tense · 4.5–6.4 Elevated · 6.5–8.4 Danger · 8.5–10 Brink. If a live source fails, its last value is kept and marked stale.
